Before You Reach Out

Having a few details ready can help make your conversation more productive and make it easier to determine the appropriate next step.

  • The property address, if you are asking about a specific property
  • Whether you are buying, selling, or renting
  • A brief description of your question or goal
  • Any important deadlines or timeframes you are working with
  • Relevant documents or information you have already received
Quick Tip: You do not need to have everything figured out before reaching out. A simple explanation of your situation is enough to start the conversation and determine the next appropriate step.
